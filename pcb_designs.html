PCB Designs

This section showcases various Printed Circuit Board (PCB) designs I have worked on. These designs include power monitoring ICs (ADE9153A, RN8209C) integrated with microcontrollers like ESP32, as well as mini power supply units.

Highlights:

  • Designed multi-layer PCBs using Altium Designer.
  • Integrated various power monitoring ICs and microcontrollers.
  • Developed compact and efficient power supply unit designs.
  • Focused on signal integrity and power delivery network optimization.
