About

Research that ships

I'm a machine learning researcher with a background in electrical engineering. My master's work at Sejong University focuses on multi-task deep learning for joint beamforming and localization in massive MIMO systems, published in IEEE Access and the Journal of Communications and Networks.

Today I work as a hardware test engineer at Friendcom, testing smart energy meters and building STM32 test tools for DLMS communication.

I enjoy taking models all the way to something usable: end-to-end pipelines, network architectures (CNN, LSTM, Transformer, Autoencoder), LLM agents with tools and memory, and the PCBs and firmware that put intelligence on real devices.

What I work on

  • Deep learning for ISAC, beamforming & localization
  • LLM agents, RAG and voice assistants
  • Embedded firmware (STM32, ESP32) & PCB design
  • Smart energy meter testing (EMC, environmental, DLMS)
  • Time-series prediction & fault detection
